Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the information you provide, such as your name and email address. As you use the app, we also store the expense records you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve Spendly: authenticating your account, displaying your expense history, and generating spending summaries and category breakdowns. We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal financial data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) solely to operate the app, and any such providers are bound to protect your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
